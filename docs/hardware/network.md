After many years of running various routers with DD-WRT or the ASUS Merlin firmware I finally splurged and got some Ubuquiti equipment, which I've been very happy with.

### Equipment

- Dream Router 7 - This thing rocks. I can do VLANs for traffic isolation, local DNS, and firewall management all with ease and without a monthly subscription for anything. The built in ad blocking isn't great and one of the four ethernet ports is blocked off for WAN use, but beyond that I can't complain.
- USW 2.5G Flex switches - I have one for each of my VLANs. Currently not doing much with them outside of traffic isolation for things on the public VLAN, but eventually I'd like to isolate my devices (printer, Rokus, etc) from the rest of the network as well. Problem is Rokus get cranky when I do that.
- FUTURE - Unifi doorbell and backdoor cameras. Will need to do some cable running for this.
